00 Context

Vela is currently a single-tenant analyst-rating-to-options-trade recommendation engine running locally and on a Hetzner VPS. It already has substantial product surface — daily recommendations with stacked conviction scores, on-demand Research, Options Lab (deep greeks), Industries view, Multibagger Scanner, Charts, Positions tracker, Knowledge page — but zero multi-tenancy. There is no users table, no auth, no per-user data scoping. All tables are globally shared.

This document defines the strategy: who it's for, what gets personalized, how to package and price it, what minimum technical work is needed, the unit economics, the KPIs, the risks, and the 90-day / 6-month / 12-month roadmap. It is grounded in a factual inventory of the current codebase so the plan is buildable rather than aspirational.

North Star
Convert Vela from a single-tenant prototype into a paying SaaS by shipping the minimum viable multi-tenant retrofit, the highest-leverage personalization wedges, and a credible accuracy story — within 90 days of kickoff.

01 Positioning & ICP

Who pays for this, and why they pay us rather than the four tools they already have open.

Primary ICP — the "serious-but-self-directed" retail options trader

$25k–$250k brokerage account, 2–10 options positions per week, already pays for one tool in the $30–$150/mo bracket (Barchart Premier, Trendspider, Optionstrat Pro, Market Chameleon). Comfortable with greeks and conviction scores. They have a broker, charts, and a screener — they're buying signal synthesis, not raw data.

Secondary ICP — the "informed swing-trader / part-time investor"

Wants a curated daily watchlist with a clear BUY/HOLD/SELL verdict; doesn't want to babysit twelve dashboards. Lower ARPU, higher volume; matches the multibagger scanner persona.

Tertiary (B2B)

Independent RIAs, family-office juniors, finance newsletter operators. Want white-label or syndication.

Explicitly NOT for
Day-traders/scalpers (latency wrong) — pure passive ETF investors (wrong instrument) — prop desks (wrong data tier).

The Wedge
A single conviction score per ticker per day fusing 12 signal classes (analyst, earnings, technical, drawdown, reversal, flow, greeks, FinBERT news, geopolitical, insider, OHLC patterns, RS) — tied to a concrete options strike recommendation. Nobody listed ships the full chain "here's why we like NVDA today AND here is the 30-delta call." The Industries module and Multibagger Scanner are adjacent surfaces no single competitor bundles. The bundling + the cross-signal model are the moat.

Honest caveat: the 100-ticker universe is small relative to Trade Ideas / Unusual Whales. Pitch is quality over breadth, opinionated over neutral. This must be reinforced in marketing.

Each: what gets personalized · why a user pays · shipping effort.

Watchlist & Universe EFFORT · S

Why pay: nobody scrolls past 92 irrelevant names.

Risk Profile EFFORT · M

Conservative / Balanced / Aggressive (or fine-tuned weights), adjusts conviction stack and strike recommender.

Why pay: turns one engine into "my engine."

Notifications & Alerts EFFORT · M-L

Email + in-app + (later) SMS/Discord/webhook for rec changes, conviction breaches, earnings T-N, unusual flow, news/FinBERT spikes, insider filings.

Why pay: THE retention loop — pulls users back daily.

Custom Signal Stacks EFFORT · M

Toggle/weight individual signals per user; weight vector consumed by stacker.

Why pay: power users want to express a thesis; differentiator vs. black-box competitors.

Saved Scans / Charts / Research EFFORT · S-M

Persist Research, Deep Options, Scanner snapshots, chart configs with tier-based retention.

Why pay: research is expensive to recompute; saved state is sticky.

Industry Weighting EFFORT · S

Boost/mute sectors in dashboard ranking. Per-user sector multiplier at view time.

Why pay: small but reduces friction.

Position-Aware Recs EFFORT · M

Recs that know your book: flag conflicting signals on held names, suggest hedges when conviction inverts, propose roll/close strikes when DTE/delta cross thresholds.

Why pay: highest-leverage differentiator nobody else does well.

Custom Universe Slots EFFORT · M

Inject N tickers into the shared pipeline (tier-gated, globally deduped).

Why pay: high-intent traders want their names covered.

Personalized AM Digest EFFORT · M

7am email/push: "Today on YOUR watchlist: 2 upgrades, 1 conviction breach, 1 earnings tomorrow, 1 unusual flow, here's the suggested strike."

Why pay: this IS the product for the secondary ICP.

★ Personalized Backtest EFFORT · L

Replay user's position history vs. what the rec said at the time.

Why pay: killer retention story for power users at the high tier.

05 Architectural Roadmap to v1 SaaS

Today: zero multi-tenancy. Seven non-negotiables to charge anyone.

  1. Auth + users table + bearer-token middleware. FastAPI dependency on every protected route. Use a hosted provider (Clerk, Supabase Auth, Auth0) — skip building password reset / 2FA / email verification yourself.
  2. user_id FK retrofit on user-owned tables: watchlist, positions, strike_snapshots, research_results, deep_options_analyses, multibagger_universe, plus a new chart_configs table. Migration writes existing rows to a single "legacy" admin user.
  3. Pipeline-amortization architecture — the unit-economics keystone:
    • Shared pipeline runs once daily over the global ~100-ticker universe; writes to globally-readable tables.
    • Per-user surfaces are filtered views over those tables.
    • Per-user on-demand compute (Research, Deep Options, Scanner, custom-universe injection) is gated by credit balance.
    • Custom universe slots are globally cached — if user A and user B both add NVDA, it's one pipeline run.
  4. Credits + quotas + entitlements service — small table layer: subscriptions, credit_balances, credit_ledger, entitlements. Every gated endpoint calls check_and_decrement(user, feature).
  5. Stripe (or Paddle) integration — checkout, customer portal, webhook → entitlement updater, dunning. Paddle handles VAT/sales-tax globally; Stripe is more flexible. Pick one.
  6. Frontend auth + tier-aware UI — login, signup, account/billing page, "upgrade to unlock" modal, credit-balance widget.
  7. Minimum-viable alerts — email-only, daily-digest + rec-change. Postmark or SES + a worker. Push/SMS/Discord defer. Even this minimum is non-negotiable for retention.
Defer Explicitly
White-label theming · multi-account orgs · full RBAC · public API with key management · mobile app · real-time websockets · anything fancier than email alerts · full audit log · backtest engine · tax-doc exports.

Stack additions: Redis (rate-limit + alert-dedup + queue), ARQ/Celery/RQ worker for alerts and on-demand pipelines, Sentry, PostHog (or similar) for product analytics. Avoid microservice split; keep monolithic FastAPI.

08 Risks & Mitigations

Regulatory · investment-adjacent
Vela gives buy/sell recs on specific securities and specific options strikes. Could shade toward "investment advice" under SEC/FINRA rules.

Mitigation: position as informational/research/educational; ship robust disclaimers ("not financial advice; informational only; past performance does not predict future results"); avoid suitability phrasing ("for YOU"); never manage or execute trades.

REQUIRES SECURITIES COUNSEL BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH — do not fabricate compliance language.
  • Data licensing — FMP / Finnhub / NewsAPI redistribution. TOS likely restricts re-display of raw data and may restrict derived data. Shared pipeline is fine; an external API tier or syndication likely needs higher commercial license. Mitigation: read each provider's commercial-use TOS; contact sales before launching API/syndication SKUs; structure public surface as derived signals + commentary, not raw quotes.
  • Trust / accuracy. A few high-profile bad calls can sink retention. Mitigation: publish accuracy stats transparently; never imply precision the model doesn't have; always show why (signal breakdown), not just what.
  • Universe is small (100 tickers). Competitors flex on breadth. Mitigation: lean into curation as a feature; use custom-universe slots as scaling pressure-release.
  • API rate-limits / single-vendor concentration. FMP is binding. Mitigation: abstract data layer so Polygon / Tiingo / IEX Cloud can sub in; pre-negotiate the upgrade tier so it's a checkbox, not a re-architecture.
  • Competitive response. Unusual Whales / Benzinga could ship "conviction stack" in a month. Mitigation: moat isn't the algorithm — it's curated universe + personalization + position-aware reasoning + Industries. Ship faster than they can copy.
  • Churn from market downturns. Options-trader populations contract sharply in bad markets. Mitigation: annual plans; secondary swing-trader ICP is more durable; multibagger surface is a partial hedge.
  • Signal-model staleness. Models drift. Mitigation: quarterly recalibration cadence; track accuracy KPIs as leading indicator.

11 Verification

v1 launch passes when these all work cleanly in the deployed environment.

  1. Sign up via auth provider → user row created → entitlements row created at Free tier → email verification works.
  2. Upgrade to Pro via Stripe Checkout → webhook fires → entitlements upgraded → dashboard immediately reflects new tier (no logout required).
  3. Run Research as Free user → blocked with 402-style upgrade prompt → upgrade → run succeeds → credit decremented → ledger row written → balance widget updates in <2s.
  4. Trigger downgrade (Stripe cancellation in test mode) → webhook fires → Pro features lock at end of period → over-quota state is graceful (read-only of past results, not 500 errors).
  5. Personalization end-to-end — add 5 watchlist tickers → set Aggressive risk profile → set "alert me when conviction crosses +60" → confirm dashboard filters to those 5 → confirm strike recommender returns aggressive-profile strikes → confirm a synthetic conviction breach triggers an email within 5 minutes.
  6. Pipeline amortization sanity check — two test users with overlapping watchlists → confirm the shared daily pipeline runs ONCE for the union → confirm each user sees only their filtered slice.
  7. Custom universe slot — Pro user adds AAPL → next pipeline run includes AAPL exactly once → second user adding AAPL doesn't trigger a second run.
  8. Credit pack purchase via Stripe → webhook → ledger adds credit-pack rows with no expiry → consumed after subscription credits.
  9. Accuracy report KPI — backfill 30 days of recs and compute hit-rate; surface a numeric metric somewhere user-visible (even if just internal admin) before any "calibrated conviction" marketing claim.
  10. Smoke-test compliance disclaimer appears on every page that displays a recommendation, and on every email containing rec content.
Reminder
These checks validate the engineering, not go-live readiness. Marketing / legal / securities-counsel review is required separately before public launch.
